Frequently Asked Questions

How does the ATS score work?
Our system runs local text heuristics mixed with semantic Gemini analysis to check if your resume meets keyword density, layout guidelines, and job criteria.
Will my uploaded resumes remain private?
Absolutely. We store resumes securely in database collections and restrict access only to the authenticated uploader.
Can I export my optimized resume?
Yes, our Resume Builder renders your optimized resume into an ATS-friendly A4 grid that you can directly print or export as PDF.
